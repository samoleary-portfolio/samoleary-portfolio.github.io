This digital portfolio is a collation of my academic and professional work.

I am a senior at Middlebury College pursuing a Major in Geography and Minor in Russian. Last year, I embarked on an independent research project in which I performed spatial analysis in R and QGIS to delineate megalopolitan counties in the United States and explore the economic advantages of megalopolitan urban structures. I presented the results of this project at the American Association of Geographers' annual meeting in San Francisco this past March, and am in the process of creating a publishable manuscript. I also served as a Teaching Assistant for two technical geography classes this past semester, assisting students and provided technical feedback with QGIS and R Coursework. This summer, I was an intern at the Vermont Agency for Natural Resources, where I utilized my skills in data science and GIS to better manage Vermont's road data database.

This coming semester, I will be taking classes that will refine my technical abilities in both statistics and geography as I look forward towards future employment opportunities and graduate school. I will also continue to serve as a Teaching Assistant for Middlebury's Introductory GIS course.

I am passionate about helping others, and am eager to find opportunities to apply my knowledge of geographical concepts and learn more about the field as a whole.
